Privacy Policy

FinLit · Effective October 25, 2026

FinLit ("FinLit," "we," "us") is a gamified financial-literacy education app, available on iPhone and on the web. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Contact info: your email address.
  • Profile info: the first name and last initial you provide.
  • Authentication data: if you sign up with Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and email Apple/Google share with us to create your account. We never see or store your Apple or Google password.
  • App activity: your lesson progress, quiz answers, XP, streaks, badges, and in-app coin balance — all generated by using the app.
  • NC Economics & Personal Finance course progress: if you use the NC EPF course, the answers you give and how they were scored, which lessons you have finished, and the standards you tell us your class has covered. This is stored with your account so your progress follows you between devices. Mid-lesson typed answers and short written responses stay on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Problem reports: if you use "Report a problem" in the NC EPF course, the reason you pick, any note you choose to write (up to 500 characters), which question or lesson it was about, and the app and content version. Reports are used only to fix course content, and they are never visible to other users.
  • Investing simulator data: your activity in the investing game (trades, portfolio value). This is entirely simulated — no real money, brokerage account, or real market data is involved.
  • Budgeting and savings data: if you use the Budget Builder or Savings Goals tools, the monthly income, spending categories, and savings targets you choose to enter. These are self-reported figures you provide for your own planning — we don't retrieve them from, or connect them to, any real bank or brokerage account.
  • Social features: if you add friends or appear on a leaderboard, your display name, level, and streak are visible to other users you connect with.
  • Push notification token: only if you enable notifications, so we can send you lesson reminders.

Guest mode

You can use FinLit without creating an account by choosing "Try as guest." Guest mode is designed so you don't have to give us anything that identifies you, and so nothing is kept after you leave.

  • No email or name. We don't ask for an email address, a name, or any sign-in details. Your profile is shown as "Guest."
  • A temporary random ID. To make the app work during your session, we create a random ID that isn't connected to who you are. Your answers to the welcome questions, lesson progress, NC EPF course answers, investing-game activity, and any tool or quiz data you create are stored with that ID while you use the app.
  • Not visible to anyone else. Guests can't add friends, join leagues or squads, or use referral codes, and never appear on leaderboards or in player search.
  • No notifications. We don't collect a push notification token from guests.
  • On your device: on the web, guest data is kept in that browser tab's temporary session storage and is removed when the tab is closed. In the iPhone app, it is kept in memory only and is removed when the app is closed. Guest data is never saved to the device's long-term storage, so the next person to use a shared device won't see it.
  • Deleted when you leave. Choosing Profile → Settings → Exit guest mode permanently deletes your guest ID and everything stored with it. If you close the tab or app without exiting, it is deleted automatically within about a day of when your guest session started.
  • Doesn't carry over. Guest progress isn't transferred if you later create an account.

Like most websites and apps, our infrastructure provider may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request times) for security and reliability. These aren't used to identify guests.

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't access your camera, photos, contacts, or location.
  • We don't link to, access, or pull data from any real bank or brokerage account. The investing feature is a game with simulated money; any income, budget, or savings figures you enter into the Budget Builder or Savings Goals tools are typed in by you, not retrieved from a real account.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the app: to authenticate you, save and sync your progress, power the social and leaderboard features you choose to use, and — if you opt in — send local or push reminders about your learning streak.

How we store and protect your information

Your data is stored with Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, which acts on our behalf and does not use your data for its own purposes. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in plain text. In the iPhone app, your session is encrypted and stored in your device's secure keychain. On the web, a signed-in session is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. Guest sessions are handled as described in "Guest mode" above.

Your choices

  • Exit guest mode: at any time, from Profile → Settings → Exit guest mode. This permanently deletes the guest session and its data.
  • Delete your account: at any time, from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and associated data and cannot be undone.
  • Notifications: you can disable reminders at any time in your device's notification settings.
  • Access or questions: email us (below) to ask what data we hold about you or to request a correction.

Children's privacy

FinLit is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. Guest mode is also intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
